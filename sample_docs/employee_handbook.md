# Lumenfield Technologies Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all permanent employees of Lumenfield Technologies (a fictional company used for this demonstration). Contractors should read the Contractor Guidelines instead.

## 1. Working Hours

Lumenfield operates flexible working hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may arrange their day with their manager.

## 2. Annual Leave

Permanent employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.83 days per month of service.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## 4. Parental Leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Remote employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours and must use a company-managed device.

Working from outside India for more than 30 consecutive days requires written approval from both HR and Legal.

Contractors are not covered by this section; their remote work rules are described in the Contractor Guidelines.

## 6. Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted for reimbursement within 30 days of being incurred, with itemised receipts. Purchases of equipment or services are governed by the Finance procurement policy.

## 7. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics line.
