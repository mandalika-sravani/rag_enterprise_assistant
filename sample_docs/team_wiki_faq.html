Wi-Fi

Staff should connect to the LF-Staff network using their SSO credentials. Visitors can use the LF-Guest network; the guest password is printed at reception and changes every Monday.

Cafeteria

The cafeteria on the ground floor is open from 08:00 to 20:00 on working days.

Printing

Printers are on every floor. Release print jobs by tapping your badge on the printer.
