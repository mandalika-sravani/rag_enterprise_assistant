# Contractor Guidelines

These guidelines apply to all contractors engaged by Lumenfield Technologies, whether through an agency or directly. Where these guidelines and a contractor's statement of work (SOW) differ, the SOW takes precedence.

## Remote Work for Contractors

Contractors may work fully remotely unless their statement of work requires on-site presence. Contractors must work on a Lumenfield-managed laptop or through the company virtual desktop (VDI); personal devices may not be used to access company systems.

## Leave and Time Off

Contractors are not covered by the employee leave policies. Time off is governed by the contractor's SOW. Contractors must notify their engagement manager at least 5 working days before any planned absence.

## System Access

Contractor accounts are created with the minimum access needed for the engagement and expire automatically on the end date of the SOW. Contractors do not receive access to HR or Finance systems.

## Equipment Return

All company equipment must be returned to the IT desk within 3 working days of the engagement ending.
