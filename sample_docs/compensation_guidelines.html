Engineering Salary Bands

Annual base salary bands for engineering roles (in lakh rupees per annum, LPA):

LevelTitleBand
L1Associate Engineer8 to 12 LPA
L2Engineer12 to 20 LPA
L3Senior Engineer20 to 32 LPA
L4Staff Engineer32 to 48 LPA

Performance Bonus

The annual performance bonus target is 10% of base salary for levels L1 to L3 and 15% of base salary for L4 and above. Payouts range from 0% to 150% of target depending on the performance rating.

Review Cycle

Compensation reviews take place once a year in April. Off-cycle adjustments require approval from the HR Director and the department head.
